About the studio · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Little by little, จากน้อยไปมาก

The Yoga Studio by Nan is a yoga school in central Udon Thani, led by Kru Nan (ครูแนน). Our studio is bright, calm and simple: a brick wall, big windows, mats and blocks ready for you.

We teach yoga for health and good posture. Just starting? Don't worry. You can practise alongside everyone else, because we give options for every pose. As we like to say, the best asana is the one that suits your body.

“อาสนะที่ดีที่สุด
คืออาสนะที่เหมาะกับเรา”
  • Beginners welcome. Every pose has an easier option
  • Limited places per class, so book your mat in advance
  • Private one-to-one sessions at times that suit you

Classes · คลาสของเรา

Find your practice

Group classes run on a monthly timetable, and private classes are booked around you. Call or message us for this month's schedule.

Beginner Class

คลาสพื้นฐาน

Learn the foundations safely: breathing, alignment and how to get into each pose without strain.

02

Flow Yoga

โฟลว์โยคะ

Poses linked with the breath into a steady, moving flow. Build strength, balance and stamina.

03

Sivananda

ศิวะนันทะ

A classical style with a steady sequence of postures, breathing and relaxation.

04

Private Class

ไพรเวทคลาส

One-to-one sessions at a time you choose, focused on your goals: easing your limitations, improving posture or counteracting long office hours.
